Research

Translational Pharmaceutical Formulation Development

Design and optimization of clinically translatable oral, injectable and transdermal drug delivery systems, including enhancer-assisted oral absorption of macromolecular therapeutics, conversion of intravenous formulations toward subcutaneous administration, and delivery across physiological barriers.

Therapeutic Discovery & Development

Fundamental and translational therapeutic development emphasizing small molecules, oligonucleotide therapeutics and mRNA-based therapies, with interests spanning autoimmune diseases, cancers, metabolic disorders, drug-induced toxicities and aging-associated diseases.
