Publications

First-author & Co-first-author Publications

Tian M, Wang L, Liu Z, Cao D, Yu C, Bennett JL, Bliss-Moreau E, Wang C, Kang DD, Xue Y, Zhong Y, Wang S, Li H, Hou X, Deng B, Dong Y
Nature Nanotechnology
Second-round revision submitted

We developed HX1 LNPs for CNS RNA delivery by screening approved CNS-related drugs using PK and safety profiles. HX1 enabled brain delivery of mRNA and Cas9 mRNA/sgRNA for gene expression and gene editing in mice, and was further validated in non-human primates using luciferase mRNA reporter and basic biosafety studies.

This project won Phase 2 and Phase 3a of the NIH TARGETED Genome Editor Delivery Challenge and is now one of four teams in the Phase 3b final round.

Tian M*, Liu Z*, Bennett JL, Bliss-Moreau E, Kang DD, Cao D, Yu C, Xue Y, Zhong Y, Wang S, Li H, Hou X, Dong Y
Nature Biomedical Engineering
First-round revision submitted

We developed NX8, a small-molecule oral permeation enhancer derived from OTC naproxen. NX8 improved oral delivery of diverse biologics, including ASOs, peptides, insulin, and heparin, achieving approximately 5–10% oral bioavailability in tested models. The platform was further validated in non-human primates using oral semaglutide tablets, with no overt treatment-associated toxicity under the tested conditions.

NX8 has received external interest; further translation and development are ongoing.

Liu Z*, Tian M*, Yu C*, Cao D, Bennett JL, Bliss-Moreau E, Hou X, Li H, Wang S, Xue Y, Zhong Y, Markovic T, Nestler JE, Dong Y
Science Translational Medicine
First-round revision submitted

Building on the same PK-guided CNS ligand-screening platform, we identified sertraline as a highly developable ligand for antibody transport. Sertraline transporter-mediated conjugates increased antibody exposure in the brain, enhanced IgG delivery in mice and non-human primates, and enabled therapeutic antibody delivery in glioblastoma and Parkinson’s disease models.

Cao D*, Tian M*, Yu C*, Xue Y*, Liu Z, Zhong Y, Hou X, Li H, Zheng YY, Wang S, Tankelevich M, Naik S, Mehandru S, Dong Y
Nature Communications (2026)
In principle accepted

We developed a glucocorticoid-derived LNP platform for localized rectal delivery of mRNA therapeutics in inflammatory bowel disease. Rectal administration preferentially delivered mRNA to inflamed colonic tissue with limited systemic exposure, showed efficacy in acute and chronic colitis models, and was further evaluated in ex vivo colonic biopsies from patients with IBD.

You M*, Tian M*, Song Z, Liu Z, Yang B, Zhang S*
Selection of GalNAc-Conjugated siKeap1 as Disease-Specific Delivery System for Chemotherapy-Induced Liver Injury and Chronic Liver Disease
Nano Letters (2024)
DOI

We compared LNP and GalNAc platforms for siRNA-mediated Keap1 silencing. GalNAc-siKeap1 provided hepatocyte-selective activity, reduced chemotherapy-induced liver injury, and preserved anticancer efficacy, highlighting the value of disease-specific delivery.

Tian M*, Mu X*, Fan D, Liu Z, Liu Q, Yue K, Song Z, Luo J*, Zhang S*
A Transformable Mucoadhesive Microgel Network for Noninvasive Multimodal Imaging And Radioprotection of a Large Area of the Gastrointestinal Tract
Advanced Materials (2023)
DOI

We developed Bi-GLUE, a transformable mucoadhesive microgel network combining high flowability with strong mucosal adhesion. The platform enabled large-area gastrointestinal X-ray and MRI imaging in murine and porcine models and reduced gastrointestinal radiation injury.

The fluidic large-area GI coating concept was subsequently extended by other groups to oral biologic delivery.

Tian M, Fan D, Liu Z, Mu X, Tao Q, Yu C*, Zhang S*
Oral Supramolecular Adsorbent for Preventing Chemo-Induced Gastrointestinal Mucositis and Microbial Dysbiosis and for Enhancing Chemoimmunotherapy
Advanced Materials (2022)
DOI

We developed SPORA-SN9, an orally administered supramolecular adsorbent that selectively captures residual chemotherapeutic drugs in the gastrointestinal tract. It reduced mucositis and microbiota disruption while preserving systemic anticancer treatment and improving chemoimmunotherapy efficacy.

This localized GI drug-scavenging concept was subsequently adopted and discussed by other groups as a broader barrier-guided detoxification strategy.

Tian M*, Xing R*, Guan J, Yang B, Zhao X, Yang J, Zhan C*, Zhang S*
A Nano Antidote Alleviates Glioblastoma Chemotoxicity without Efficacy Compromise
Nano Letters (2021)
DOI

We developed a nanoantidote based on an inverse-delivery concept: instead of overcoming the blood–brain barrier, we leveraged it to confine the antidote to peripheral tissues. This reduced systemic temozolomide toxicity while preserving drug exposure and antitumor efficacy in the brain.

Related nano-antidote strategies were subsequently reported by other groups, supporting the broader applicability of barrier-guided detoxification.
